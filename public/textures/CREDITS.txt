CC0 assets from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), compressed for the web. No changes to the license.

Textures: herringbone_parquet, white_stucco, curly_teddy_natural, white_oak_veneer (colour and roughness maps).

Models: potted_plant_02, potted_plant_04, modern_ceiling_lamp_01, ceramic_vase_01, wall_clock, side_table_01.

Modeled for this scene: the desk, monitor and light bar, keyboard, mouse, laptop, phone, office chair, headphones, mug, notebook, books, floating shelves, fluted sideboard, smart speaker, wood slat wall, neon sign, and photo frame. Poly Haven does not publish modern versions of these.
